Campaign link builder

Builds tagged URLs so you can tell which campaign produced each sign-up. Values are lowercased and spaces become hyphens — consistency is the whole point, since LinkedIn and linkedin would otherwise report as two separate sources.

Attribution is captured into form submissions, not an analytics dashboard. A tagged visit shows up when that person submits a form — you'll see utm_source and friends alongside their answers in Firestore.
