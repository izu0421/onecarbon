01

We found that boosting one-carbon metabolism protects the ageing brain.

Our work started at Cambridge with a fundamental biological finding. One-carbon metabolism is a pathway involved in protecting mitochondrial function and sustaining long-term brain health. It was central enough to how we think about cognitive care that it became our name.

To act on it, we built a living biological solution: a naturally occurring gut strain, isolated and refined in our laboratory, that works from within your own body.2

  • 0183% boost in neuronal health in pre-clinical cell and animal models
  • 02Grounded in 5 peer-reviewed Cambridge studies
  • 03Piloted in a small human study — 15 participants, ages 50–653
